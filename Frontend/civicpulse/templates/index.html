The three vitals we optimize for

Tracking, pruning, prevention.

The old grievance pipeline breaks down in three specific places. Our AI is built to fix exactly those three — nothing more, nothing decorative.

VITAL 01

<30sec to route

Tracking

Complaints are classified and forwarded to the right authority the instant they're filed — no manual sorting queue, no clerk deciding which desk it lands on.

VITAL 02

1 case, not 40

Pruning

NLP clustering merges near-identical complaints about the same pothole or pipeline into a single case, so officials see true scale instead of a flooded inbox.

VITAL 03

0 phone calls needed

Prevention

Every citizen can check exactly where their complaint stands, in their own language, without ever calling a helpdesk or visiting an office.

How citizens report

  • WhatsApp chat interface, no app install

  • Photo, video & audio proof upload

How the backend works

This is where the AI does the sorting a human clerk used to do by hand.

  • NLP classification by category & urgency
  • Automatic de-duplication & clustering
  • Routing to the correct authority level
  • PolicyGyaan cross-check for relevant schemes
03
Resolved

Resolution

How officials fix it

Officials get a shortlist of what actually needs their attention, and a nudge if it sits too long.

  • Actionable dashboard, ranked by cluster size
  • Periodic reminders on ageing complaints
  • DigiLocker-linked proof verification
  • One-tap approval of AI-suggested action plan
